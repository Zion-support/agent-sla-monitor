## Human review example
1. Use a fictional ticket with a two-hour response target and a recorded first response after 90 minutes.
2. A human reviewer can note 30 minutes of illustrative headroom, subject to the contract clock, pauses, time zone and business hours.
3. Record evidence, exceptions and a named owner. No automatic monitoring, escalation or contractual compliance determination occurs here.
